## The Cloud Honeymoon and the Dreaded Month-End Bill 💸

Migrating to Microsoft Azure is one of the best technological steps a company can take. You gain instant scalability and military-grade security. However, months after migration, many SMBs and executives face a common problem: **the cloud bill is much higher than projected**.

In the cloud, **you pay for what you provision and consume**. If you leave a light on in your house all month, you will pay for it. Azure works the same way. This is where **FinOps** (Cloud Financial Operations) comes to the rescue.

---

## What is FinOps and Why is it Vital for Your Business?

FinOps is a discipline that brings together Finance, Technology (IT), and Business teams to ensure that **every cent invested in the cloud generates maximum value**.

If you don't apply financial engineering to your cloud architecture, you are literally throwing money away.

## 🛠️ 4 FinOps Strategies to Optimize Your Azure Environment

### 1. Right-Sizing
Many engineers configure servers based on old physical hardware sizes. After analyzing metrics, we often find servers using less than 15% of their capacity. Adjusting the size to actual usage is like moving from a 10-bedroom mansion to the 2-bedroom apartment you actually need.

### 2. Hunting "Zombie Resources"
When you delete a VM, associated Managed Disks or public IPs are often left behind, billing every minute for doing nothing.

### 3. Leverage Reservations and Hybrid Benefits
**Azure Reservations** can offer up to **72%** discounts for 1 or 3-year commitments. Meanwhile, **Azure Hybrid Benefit** lets you reuse your current Windows Server licenses, avoiding double payments.

### 4. Tagging and Governance
![Cloud Governance and Tagging](/images/blog/Gobernanza-Etiquetado-y-Colaboración.avif "Implementing governance policies and cloud cost control")
Do you know which department spent the most this month? Without clear tags, it's impossible to tell. Accountability is key to cost control.
