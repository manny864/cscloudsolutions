The Four Clear Rules

1. First touch wins

A customer belongs to a single partner. If they came in through your link and later through another, the customer is yours.

2. Attribution at signup

Applies to new registrations. Accounts that existed before the link cannot be attributed retroactively.

3. Locked-in percentage

Your % is locked in at each payment. Future renegotiations do not alter what has already been earned.

4. No self-referrals

Signups with email domains matching the partner are not attributed. The system validates this automatically.

The limitation you should know about (and how we safeguard against it)

Automatic web attribution depends on browser cookies. Private browsing, ad blockers or switching devices can interfere. For strategic accounts or high-volume negotiations: let us know before the customer signs up and we will attribute it manually in the system. That way you have 100% certainty without depending on a cookie.

Operations and Payments

How and when you get paid your commissions

Each commission has a transparent lifecycle that you can audit in real time from your partner dashboard, no password required.

Pending

Accrued. The customer paid for the month and your commission has been recorded.

Payable

The period has closed and it is ready to enter the payout batch.

Approved

Reviewed and confirmed internally by our finance team.

Paid

Transferred to your bank account with its proof of payment.

Reversed

If the customer requested a refund and the commission was canceled or deducted.

3. Minimum Transfer We set whether there is a minimum threshold below which the balance rolls over to the next batch.
